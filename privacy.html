Privacy notice.

This notice explains how information submitted through the BARTSS project brief is used. It is intended for business enquiries and project qualification.

Information we collect

When you submit a project request, BARTSS may collect your name, business email, phone number if provided, company/brand, role, company website, indicative investment range, preferred engagement and the project problem you describe. Technical anti-abuse data such as a one-way hash of the request IP address and basic browser information may also be recorded.

Why we use it

  • To assess whether BARTSS is a suitable partner for the project.
  • To prepare and discuss an appropriate project scope.
  • To respond to your enquiry and manage the resulting commercial conversation.
  • To protect the form against spam and automated abuse.

Retention and access

Project enquiry data is accessible only to the BARTSS team responsible for business development and project evaluation. It should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the enquiry, the resulting business relationship, or applicable legal obligations.

Sharing

BARTSS does not sell project enquiry data. Information may be processed by hosting, email or infrastructure providers only where necessary to operate the website and respond to the request.

Your request

You may request access, correction or deletion of information submitted through this form by contacting hello@bartss.com. Where applicable, requests will be handled under the relevant data-protection rules, including Türkiye's KVKK requirements.

Important: Before public launch, BARTSS should add its legal entity/contact details and final retention policy to this notice.
